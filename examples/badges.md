Badge apps: one "## " section per app whose Dock badge shows on the
bar. See docs/badges.md. A line ":enabled: no" at the top turns the
badges off, and ":count_only: yes" in a section hides that app's badge
while it is a dot rather than a count. ":speak: yes" in a section reads
the app's count aloud during a break, again every minute while it lasts,
between ":speak_from:" and ":speak_until:" at the top (HH:MM, 10:00 and
22:00 by default). ":windows: yes" in a section shows a ⧉ item while
the app has two or more windows, minimized ones counted, and a click on
it cycles to the next window.
